"Go" and "go" count as two words. Callers want to choose: an option to fold case.

## Design

- `Options{Fold bool}` in a new `options.go`.
- `WordsWith(text, opt)` and `CountWith(text, opt)`: `Words` and `Count` with options. `Words` and `Count` stay as they are (no breaking change).
- Fold lowercases with `strings.ToLower` after the split, so the split rule is the same.
- README: one line for `WordsWith` / `CountWith`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] `WordsWith` with `Fold` lowercases every word; without it, it is `Words`.
- [x] `CountWith` with `Fold` counts "Go" and "go" as one word.
- [x] The README documents the option.
